What the rules require

Each statement comes from an official source, quoted on the linked page. This is a summary for orientation, not legal advice.

Coverage threshold

  • Under the current eCFR text, VEVRAA covers contracts and subcontracts of $200,000 or more. source
  • OFCCP reports that the VEVRAA threshold increased from $150,000 to $200,000. source
  • For Section 503, affirmative action program requirements apply to contractors with at least 50 employees and a single contract of $50,000 or more. source

Mandatory listing duty

  • Covered contractors must immediately list all employment openings with the appropriate employment service delivery system (ESDS) where the opening occurs. source

Where to list

  • Listing with the state workforce agency job bank, or with the local ESDS where the opening occurs, satisfies the listing requirement. source

Listing format

  • Listings must provide the vacancy information in any manner and format the ESDS permits. source

Federal contractor disclosure

  • Contractors must tell each state ESDS that they want priority referrals of protected veterans for openings at all locations in that state. source

Hiring official contact

  • Contractors must give the ESDS contact information for the official responsible for hiring at each location. source

Exempt openings

  • Executive and senior management, positions filled from within the organization, and positions lasting three days or less are excluded from all employment openings. source

Exempt locations

  • Listing requirements in paragraphs 2 and 3 do not apply to openings that occur and are filled outside the 50 states. source

Remote jobs

  • For a remote job, the contractor may list with an ESDS in any area where qualified candidates might be found. source

Privately run job services

  • Providing openings to a privately run job service or exchange satisfies the contractor listing obligation. source

Hiring vendors

  • If the contractor uses an external job search organization, it must give the ESDS that organization contact information. source

Record retention

  • Personnel and employment records must be kept for two years from the date of the record or personnel action, whichever is later. source
  • Contractors with fewer than 150 employees, or without a Government contract of at least $200,000, keep records for one year instead. source
  • Records required under 60-300.44(k), the veteran data collection, must be kept for three years. source

Executive Order 11246 revoked

  • Executive Order 11246 was revoked by Executive Order 14173 on January 21, 2025. source

Wind-down

  • Federal contractors were told to wind down compliance with the E.O. 11246 regulatory scheme by April 21, 2025. source

VEVRAA and Section 503 still apply

  • Section 503 and VEVRAA, with their implementing regulations, remain in effect. source

2026 final rule

  • The Department of Labor published a final rule revising the VEVRAA regulations on August 21, 2026. source
  • The final rule aligns the VEVRAA regulations with Executive Order 14173 and removes cross-references to the Executive Order 11246 authority. source
  • The final rule takes effect September 21, 2026. source
  • The final rule updates VEVRAA jurisdictional thresholds that the FAR Council adjusted on October 1, 2025. source

Illinois state listing

  • Illinois requires contractors to enter openings in IllinoisJobLink.com and mark them as federal contractor listings. source

Illinois threshold conflict

  • The Illinois IDES page still states a $100,000 threshold, which differs from the later OFCCP figure. source
