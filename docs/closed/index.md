# Closed, acquired and merged job boards and HR services

Entries that no longer operate independently, each with a cited source.

- Torc: Acquired by Randstad in 2024.  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randstad_Holding
- Monster: Merged with CareerBuilder in 2024. Job board merged with CareerBuilder in 2024, with Apollo-managed funds becoming majority owner; Randstad had bought Monster.com in 2016. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randstad_(company)
- Stack Overflow Jobs: Closed in 2022. Stack Overflow Jobs and Developer Story were discontinued on March 31, 2022, with no successor job board. Source: https://hackajob.com/talent/blog/the-end-of-stack-overflow-jobs-heres-what-it-means-for-you/
- Jobvite: Merged with JazzHR in 2021. Applicant tracking vendor purchased by K1 Investment Management and merged with JazzHR and NXTThing RPO. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jobvite
- GitHub Jobs: Closed in 2021. GitHub announced the deprecation of GitHub Jobs in April 2021 and sunset the site on August 19, 2021. Source: https://github.blog/changelog/2021-04-19-deprecation-notice-github-jobs-site/
- Hired: Acquired by Vettery in 2020. Tech recruitment marketplace acquired by Vettery, which was part of the Adecco Group. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hired_(company)
- Workopolis: Acquired by Indeed in 2018. Acquired by Indeed (Recruit Holdings) and shut down as an independent job board; it remains as a white label portal for Indeed's Canadian postings. The Wikipedia article gives both April and June 2018 for the deal. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workopolis
- Vettery: Acquired by Adecco Group in 2018. Recruitment marketplace startup acquired by the Adecco Group for $100 million USD. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adecco
- Glassdoor: Acquired by Recruit Holdings in 2018. Employer review and job site bought by Recruit Holdings, the owner of Indeed, for US$1.2 billion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glassdoor
- Programmed Maintenance: Acquired by Persol in 2017. Australian labour hire company acquired by Persol for $778M. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persol_Holdings
- CareerBuilder: Acquired by Apollo Global Management in 2017. Job board bought by private-equity firm Apollo Global Management, later merged with Monster.com in 2024. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CareerBuilder
- SimplyHired: Acquired by Recruit Holdings in 2016. Job search site purchased by Recruit Holdings, a competitor of Indeed in the same group. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SimplyHired
- Jobr: Acquired by Monster in 2016. Monster.com acquired the San Francisco job search app Jobr in June 2016. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2016/06/08/monster-snaps-up-tinder-for-jobs-app-jobr/
- 7S Group: Acquired by ManpowerGroup in 2015. German HR services firm acquired by ManpowerGroup for $140.4 million. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ManpowerGroup
- Identified: Acquired by Workday in 2014. Recruiting startup acquired by Workday along with its artificial intelligence technology. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Workday,_Inc.
- SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting: Acquired by SAP in 2012. HR and recruiting software vendor acquired by SAP; the acquisition was completed in February 2012. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuccessFactors
- Oracle Taleo: Acquired by Oracle in 2012. Recruiting and talent software vendor acquired by Oracle Corporation for $1.9 billion. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taleo
- Indeed: Acquired by Recruit Holdings in 2012. Job search site acquired by Japan-based Recruit Co. Ltd. and run as an independent operating unit. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indeed
- Spherion: Acquired by Randstad in 2011.  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randstad_Holding
- Vedior: Acquired by Randstad in 2008.  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randstad_Holding
- Yacht: Rebranded as Randstad Professional. Yacht now operates as Randstad Professional, which the Randstad Professional site presents as the combined brand; the page gives no year for the change. The quote was taken from a fetch summary and should be checked against the page text before publishing. Source: https://www.randstadprofessional.nl/
