Closed, acquired and merged

Job boards, staffing firms and hiring tools that no longer operate on their own: shut down, bought, merged or renamed. 21 so far, each with a cited source. Where an entry is also in the directory, its page carries a notice.

Acquired (16)

  • Torc Acquired 2024
    Acquired by Randstad. Source
  • Hired Acquired 2020
    Acquired by Vettery. Tech recruitment marketplace acquired by Vettery, which was part of the Adecco Group. Source
  • Workopolis Acquired 2018
    Acquired by Indeed. Acquired by Indeed (Recruit Holdings) and shut down as an independent job board; it remains as a white label portal for Indeed's Canadian postings. The Wikipedia article gives both April and June 2018 for the deal. Source
  • Vettery Acquired 2018
    Acquired by Adecco Group. Recruitment marketplace startup acquired by the Adecco Group for $100 million USD. Source
  • Glassdoor Acquired 2018
    Acquired by Recruit Holdings. Employer review and job site bought by Recruit Holdings, the owner of Indeed, for US$1.2 billion. Source
  • Programmed Maintenance Acquired 2017
    Acquired by Persol. Australian labour hire company acquired by Persol for $778M. Source
  • CareerBuilder Acquired 2017
    Acquired by Apollo Global Management. Job board bought by private-equity firm Apollo Global Management, later merged with Monster.com in 2024. Source
  • SimplyHired Acquired 2016
    Acquired by Recruit Holdings. Job search site purchased by Recruit Holdings, a competitor of Indeed in the same group. Source
  • Jobr Acquired 2016
    Acquired by Monster. Monster.com acquired the San Francisco job search app Jobr in June 2016. Source
  • 7S Group Acquired 2015
    Acquired by ManpowerGroup. German HR services firm acquired by ManpowerGroup for $140.4 million. Source
  • Identified Acquired 2014
    Acquired by Workday. Recruiting startup acquired by Workday along with its artificial intelligence technology. Source
  • SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting Acquired 2012
    Acquired by SAP. HR and recruiting software vendor acquired by SAP; the acquisition was completed in February 2012. Source
  • Oracle Taleo Acquired 2012
    Acquired by Oracle. Recruiting and talent software vendor acquired by Oracle Corporation for $1.9 billion. Source
  • Indeed Acquired 2012
    Acquired by Recruit Holdings. Job search site acquired by Japan-based Recruit Co. Ltd. and run as an independent operating unit. Source
  • Spherion Acquired 2011
    Acquired by Randstad. Source
  • Vedior Acquired 2008
    Acquired by Randstad. Source
