# Crelate integrations

Platforms Crelate connects to, each from a page on one party's own site.

## Multiposting and job distribution

- LogicMelon: integrates with (https://www.crelate.com/marketplace)

## Job boards and aggregators

- CareerBuilder: integrates with (https://www.crelate.com/pricing)
- Dice: integrates with (https://www.crelate.com/pricing)
- Indeed: integrates with (https://www.crelate.com/pricing)
- Monster: sends jobs to (https://www.crelate.com/marketplace)

Canonical page: https://jobboardlist.com/integrations/crelate/
