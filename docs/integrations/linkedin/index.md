# LinkedIn integrations

Platforms LinkedIn connects to, each from a page on one party's own site.

## Applicant tracking systems

- Ceipal: receives jobs from (https://www.ceipal.com/staffing-agency-software/applicant-tracking-system)
- Greenhouse: receives jobs from (https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/4405801275931-Free-job-posts)
- iCIMS: integrates with (https://marketplace.icims.com/en-US/listing?locale=en-US)
- JazzHR: integrates with (https://www.jazzhr.com/integrations)
- JobAdder: receives jobs from (https://jobadder.com/platform/operations/job-boards)
- Jobvite: integrates with (https://www.jobvite.com/marketplace/)
- Lever: integrates with (https://www.lever.co/integrations)
- Oracle Recruiting Cloud: integrates with (https://www.oracle.com/human-capital-management/recruiting/)
- Pinpoint: integrates with (https://www.pinpointhq.com/integrations)
- Recruitee: integrates with (https://recruitee.com/api-webhooks-integrations)
- Teamtailor: integrates with (https://www.teamtailor.com/en-us/integrations/)
- Tracker: integrates with (https://www.tracker-rms.com/recruitment-ats/)
- Workable: receives jobs from (https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012441868-Which-job-boards-can-I-post-to-through-Workable)

## Multiposting and job distribution

- Appcast: integrates with (https://www.appcast.io/products/appcast-one/)
- JobTarget: integrates with (https://www.jobtarget.com/job-advertising-solutions)

## Job boards and aggregators

- Job Bank: integrates with (https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/network)
