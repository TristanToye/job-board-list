# ApplicantStack integrations

Platforms ApplicantStack connects to, each from a page on one party's own site.

## Multiposting and job distribution

- JobTarget: sends jobs to (https://www.applicantstack.com/job-boards/)

## Job boards and aggregators

- Indeed: sends jobs to (https://www.applicantstack.com/job-boards/)
- ZipRecruiter: integrates with (https://www.applicantstack.com/partners/)

Canonical page: https://jobboardlist.com/integrations/applicantstack/
