Quick answers

Which job boards and aggregators does Workable connect to?

According to pages on the platforms' own sites, Workable connects to Adzuna, CareerBuilder, Careerjet, Glassdoor, Google for Jobs, Indeed, Jobsdb, JobStreet, Jooble, LinkedIn, Monster, Reed and 3 more.

Which other platforms does Workable connect to?

According to pages on the platforms' own sites, Workable connects to CV-Library Group, Jobcase, Jobsite, Recruit.net, US.Jobs, X job board.

A connection is listed only when one of the two parties states it on its own site. Missing names may still connect. See the ecosystem map for all documented connections.
