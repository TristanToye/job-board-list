Which job boards and aggregators does Pinpoint connect to?

According to pages on the platforms' own sites, Pinpoint connects to Adzuna, CareerBuilder, Careerjet, CV-Library, Glassdoor, Indeed, Jooble, LinkedIn, Monster, Naukri, Reed, Reed and 5 more.

According to pages on the platforms' own sites, Pinpoint connects to Careers In Compliance, Education Week Jobs, Give a Grad a Go, JobMinglr, Leisurejobs, myGwork, NHS Jobs, TeamWork Online, WISE.
