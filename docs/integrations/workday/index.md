# Workday Recruiting integrations

Platforms Workday Recruiting connects to, each from a page on one party's own site.

## Multiposting and job distribution

- DirectEmployers Association: integrates with (https://directemployers.org/programmatic-job-advertising/)
- Joveo: integrates with (https://www.joveo.com/partners/ats-integrations/)

## Job boards and aggregators

- Reed: sends jobs to (https://www.reed.co.uk/recruiter/ats-integration)

Canonical page: https://jobboardlist.com/integrations/workday/
