# iCIMS integrations

Platforms iCIMS connects to, each from a page on one party's own site.

## Multiposting and job distribution

- Broadbean: integrates with (https://www.broadbean.com/partners/)
- DirectEmployers Association: integrates with (https://directemployers.org/programmatic-job-advertising/)
- JobTarget: integrates with (https://www.jobtarget.com/job-advertising-solutions)
- Joveo: integrates with (https://www.joveo.com/partners/ats-integrations/)

## Job boards and aggregators

- LinkedIn: integrates with (https://marketplace.icims.com/en-US/listing?locale=en-US)
- Talent.com: sends jobs to (https://www.talent.com/ats)

Canonical page: https://jobboardlist.com/integrations/icims/
