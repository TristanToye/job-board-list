# JobAdder integrations

Platforms JobAdder connects to, each from a page on one party's own site.

## Multiposting and job distribution

- Broadbean: integrates with (https://jobadder.com/platform/operations/integrations)

## Job boards and aggregators

- Adzuna: integrates with (https://jobadder.com/platform/operations/job-boards)
- CV-Library: integrates with (https://jobadder.com/platform/operations/job-boards)
- Indeed: integrates with (https://jobadder.com/platform/operations/job-boards)
- IrishJobs: integrates with (https://jobadder.com/platform/operations/job-boards)
- LinkedIn: sends jobs to (https://jobadder.com/platform/operations/job-boards)
- Reed: integrates with (https://jobadder.com/platform/operations/job-boards)
- SEEK: integrates with (https://jobadder.com/platform/operations/job-boards)
