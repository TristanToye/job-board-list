Quick answers

What is Talent.com?

Talent.com is a job aggregator based in Canada.

Does Talent.com have an API?

According to this directory, Talent.com restricts programmatic access to approved partners. Documentation: https://www.talent.com/ats.

Which languages does Talent.com support?

This directory records Talent.com as supporting English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish.

How large is Talent.com?

Talent.com ranks #16,777 among all websites by web link popularity (Majestic Million, 2026-11-09), in the Established tier. This measures how widely the site is linked to, not traffic or job volume.

How many jobs does Talent.com list?

Talent.com reports 35,000,000 jobs available on its own site (as of 2026-11-05).

Which platforms does Talent.com connect to?

According to this directory, Talent.com receives jobs from Abler, Acquire, Allibo, Appcast, Applicantpro, Apploi and others; integrates with Pinpoint, Job Bank.
