Quick answers

What is Indeed?

Indeed is an aggregator and board based in United States. It was founded in 2004.

Who owns Indeed?

According to this directory, Indeed is owned by Recruit Holdings Co., Ltd. (subsidiary, since 2012).

Does Indeed have an API?

According to this directory, Indeed restricts programmatic access to approved partners. Documentation: https://docs.indeed.com.

Which languages does Indeed support?

This directory records Indeed as supporting English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Turkish.

How large is Indeed?

Indeed ranks #340 among all websites by web link popularity (Majestic Million, 2026-11-11), in the Major tier. This measures how widely the site is linked to, not traffic or job volume.

Which platforms does Indeed connect to?

According to this directory, Indeed receives jobs from Broadbean, Workable, Greenhouse; integrates with Oracle Recruiting Cloud, JazzHR, Pinpoint, Job Bank, Appcast, Lever.
