## Quick answers

**Which platforms does Zoho Recruit connect to?** According to this directory, Zoho Recruit sends jobs to ZipRecruiter, WhatJobs, LinkedIn, CareerBuilder; integrates with Monster APAC, TimesJobs, Dice, Gigajob, Indeed, Adzuna and others.
