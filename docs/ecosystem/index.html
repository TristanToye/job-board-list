The hiring ecosystem map

Employers manage hiring in an applicant tracking system, which pushes jobs to boards and aggregators directly or through distribution platforms, and candidates find them there. This map shows the 184 connections documented on the platforms' own pages, 86 of them between entries in this directory.

Solid arrows mean the first party sends jobs to the second. Dashed lines mean the two integrate in a way the page does not describe as a job feed. A missing line does not mean no connection, only that we have not found one stated. Hover a name to highlight its connections.
