# Competitor directories and resources for the hiring technology ecosystem

Research date: 12 November 2026. About 19 search and fetch calls. Several primary pages could not be fetched (VONQ channel catalogue 404, jobboardsearch.com 403), so some items rely on search snippets and are flagged.

## 1. Who serves "list of job boards", "job board directory", "ATS integrations", "multiposting", "outplacement firms", "HR tech directory"?

### Takeaway
The intent is split across many partial players: dedicated job board directories (several small, paid or now closed), ATS vendor marketplaces (vendor scoped), multiposter networks (sold by count, not by name), software review sites (G2, Capterra), analyst landscape maps (Talent Tech Labs, AIM Group, often gated) and vendor written listicles (outplacement). The biggest neutral job board encyclopedia, Jobboardfinder, has closed in 2026, which leaves an open gap.

### Cited Findings

Dedicated job board directories
- Jobboardfinder has shut down: its site now returns a 410 removal notice reading "Jobboardfinder ferme ses portes". At its peak it listed 993 job boards across 244 countries and territories, running 2014 to 2026. Profiles had comparisons, ratings and reviews, and partner sites could claim profiles to update rates and audience figures (a paid partner model with billing and automatic renewal, now ended; account data was deleted). Owner was the Konduco group (also owns Gestmax ATS, AD-Men agency CRM, Koopt referral, Aktor Interactive recruitment marketing agency). Source: [jobboardfinder.com](https://www.jobboardfinder.com/)
- A Green Jobs UK page still describes Jobboard Finder as a "job board encyclopedia" of 850+ job boards with positioning, marketing activities, media partners, audience figures, offer counts, products and prices, which the team said it checked. This page is now stale given the closure. Source: [greenjobs.co.uk](https://www.greenjobs.co.uk/jobboard-finder.cms.asp)
- Onrec runs a UK leaning "Job Board Directory: UK & Specialist Job Sites". The section page shows 25 listings per page with about 90 pages of pagination (roughly 2,250 entries, my estimate), mixing job boards, "recruiter suppliers" and "job-seeker suppliers". Listings are bare (name plus profile link, no description, location or dates on the index) and the site links to a "Price Plans" page, suggesting paid listing tiers. Search results expose deep paginated URLs (for example ?page=30, ?page=47) as the ranking page type. Source: [onrec.com/directory/job-boards](https://www.onrec.com/directory/job-boards); search snippet count of about 980 listings from [onrec.com](https://www.onrec.com/directory/job-boards/cwjobscouk?page=2) conflicts with the pagination estimate.
- JobBoardSearch (jobboardsearch.com) is a meta directory of job boards; Postmake says it includes 443 job boards. It ranks with many programmatic keyword pages (for example "finance+type filter", "entry level+opportunities at top companies"). Could not fetch the site (403). Source: [Postmake](https://postmake.io/tools/jobboardsearch); [jobboardsearch.com example page](https://jobboardsearch.com/finance+type-filter)
- CareerPanels claims over 4,765 job boards and job search sites in the United States (Product Hunt claim, unverified, US only). Source: [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/careerpanels)
- Job Board Secrets sells a paid directory of "the world's top 1,000 job boards" on Gumroad. Source: [Gumroad](https://jobboardsecrets.gumroad.com/l/jobboards)

Wikipedia and Wikidata
- Wikipedia "List of employment websites" has only about 51 rows with four columns (Site, Location, Type, Notes), inconsistent types, only two references across the whole table, and a "relies on a single source" banner with citation-needed categories dating from September 2024. Last edited 14 October 2026, semi-protected. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_employment_websites)
- A Wikidata 2025 workshop paper examines how Wikidata models HR concepts (employment, education, skills), but I found no well-developed job board class or dataset. Source: [CEUR-WS Vol-4108 paper 8](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4108/paper8.pdf); [Wikidata WikiProject Occupations](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:WikiProject_Occupations)

Industry associations
- IAEWS (International Association of Employment Web Sites) described itself in 2010 as the trade association of the online employment industry, representing about 60,000 employment sites, with corporate HR and staffing firms ineligible for membership. Source: [ICANN letter archive](https://forum.icann.org/lists/jobs-phased-allocation/msg00185.html)
- In 2014 it had 900 member organisations, founded by Peter Weddle. Source: [College Recruiter blog, 2014](https://www.collegerecruiter.com/blog/2014/02/05/college-recruiter-ceo-faith-rothberg-elected-iaews-board-governors)
- No public member directory and no recent (2025 or 2026) activity surfaced in searches. Most recent mentions are old Onrec member announcements. Source: [Onrec](https://www.onrec.com/node/101749); [ZipRecruiter blog](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/blog/ziprecruiter-iaews-board-of-governors/)

Analyst and research landscapes

These are image infographics plus a taxonomy, hosted on an Allegis Group domain. Source: [hrtechfeed](https://hrtechfeed.com/?p=434079); [finanzen.net](https://www.finanzen.net/nachricht/aktien/talent-tech-labs-releases-first-extended-workforce-technology-ecosystem-15272290); [TTL taxonomy page](https://www.talenttechlabs.com.prod.allegisgroup.com/en/insights/ecosystem-taxonomy)

- Careerminds has staging subdomains (stg-uk, stg-ca, stg-sg, stg.) indexed in search, showing heavy programmatic list building. Source: [search result URLs](https://stg.careerminds.com/list-of-outplacement-firms)

### Inferences
- Jobboardfinder's 2026 closure removes the only global, structured, multi country job board encyclopedia (about 993 boards, 244 countries). Its former users (employers, agencies, job board owners) and its backlinks are now orphaned; this is the single clearest opening for Job Board Atlas.
- No competitor combines job boards, ATS, multiposters, staffing and outplacement in one linked graph; each covers one slice.
- The outplacement SERP is captured by self ranking vendor listicles, so a neutral cited listing could stand out.

### Gaps
- Exact current ranking positions for the target queries were not measured (no SERP tool beyond US web search).
- Idibu, JobG8, Appcast publisher networks, SelectHub, GetApp, Josh Bersin research, public-apis and API directories were not checked within the call budget.
- IAEWS current status unconfirmed.

## 2. What each competitor holds, freshness, citation, openness, structure, SEO footprint

### Takeaway
Data is either small and open but uncited (Wikipedia), large but paid or claim to update (Jobboardfinder, Onrec, Job Board Secrets), vendor scoped (ATS marketplaces, multiposters), or gated analysis (TTL, AIM Group, Cielo). None publishes per fact citations.

### Cited Findings
- Wikipedia: about 51 entries, open licence, but citations on only two rows. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_employment_websites)
- Jobboardfinder: 993 boards, 244 countries, partner paid profile updates, closed 2026. Source: [jobboardfinder.com](https://www.jobboardfinder.com/)
- Onrec: large UK centric list, bare listings, price plans, no update dates visible. Source: [Onrec](https://www.onrec.com/directory/job-boards)
- Job Board Secrets: 1,000 boards, paid download. Source: [Gumroad](https://jobboardsecrets.gumroad.com/l/jobboards)
- TTL: 500+ TA vendors and 346 extended workforce vendors as infographics, no per vendor facts or links in the map. Source: [hrtechfeed](https://hrtechfeed.com/?p=434079)
- AIM Group: revenue and traffic rankings in paid annual report. Source: [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/recruitment-marketplaces-the-great-resignation-to-the-great-move-forward-301545152.html)
- Greenhouse, Lever and similar marketplaces: per partner pages (integrations.greenhouse.com/partners/...) that rank for "X + Greenhouse integration" queries, but only list their own partners. Source: [Greenhouse Broadbean partner page](https://integrations.greenhouse.com/partners/broadbean)
- Multiposters publish network counts (7,000+, 25,000+) rather than named, browsable lists. Source: [Broadbean](https://broadbean.com/); [Lever help](https://help.lever.co/hc/en-us/articles/20087302685213)
- G2 and Capterra rank through category, segment and country clone pages driven by reviews and paid vendor placement. Source: [G2](https://www.g2.com/categories/job-board); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/job-board-software/)

### Inferences
- Integration pairs ("ATS X with job board Y", "ATS X public API") are documented only inside each vendor's own marketplace or help centre. A cross vendor integration matrix with citations would be unique.
- Programmatic page types that rank today: per board profile pages, category or country pages, paginated lists, "X integration" partner pages and "top N firms" listicles. Job Board Atlas can match these with cited entity pages.

### Gaps
- Traffic estimates for competitor sites (Similarweb, Semrush) were not gathered.
- Monetisation of JobBoardSearch and CareerPanels not confirmed.

## 3. Recurring gaps and where an open, cited, neutral directory could be better

### Takeaway
Recurring weaknesses are missing citations, vendor bias, paywalls or gating, stale or abandoned data, no API or integration detail, no ownership mapping, and UK or US skew. Job Board Atlas's cited facts, API and partner program fields and ownership connections address each.

### Cited Findings
- No citations: Wikipedia list relies on a single source. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_employment_websites)
- Vendor bias: outplacement lists rank the author first; Jobboardfinder was owned by an HR tech group with its own ATS and agency. Source: [Careerminds](https://careerminds.com/top-outplacement-firms-list); [jobboardfinder.com](https://www.jobboardfinder.com/)
- Abandonment and staleness: Jobboardfinder closed; Cielo Navigator shows launch era coverage only; IAEWS shows no recent activity. Source: [jobboardfinder.com](https://www.jobboardfinder.com/); [Cielo](https://resources.cielotalent.com/recruiting-technology-navigator/)
- Paywalls and gating: Job Board Secrets, AIM Group report, Cielo form gate. Source: [Gumroad](https://jobboardsecrets.gumroad.com/l/jobboards); [Cielo PR](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/cielo-introduces-new-recruiting-tech-navigator-to-help-explore-the-recruiting-technology-marketplace-815891399.html)
- Conflicting counts with no methodology: Greenhouse 452 vs 525; Kombo 86 vs 100+; Broadbean 7,000 vs 5,000. Source: [pin.com](https://www.pin.com/blog/best-greenhouse-integrations/); [Greenhouse](https://greenhouse.com/integrations); [Kombo](https://www.kombo.dev/vs/merge); [MHR](https://mhrglobal.com/uk/en/partners/broadbean)
- Ownership consolidation is real but undocumented in directories, for example the CareerBuilder and Monster merger cited only in a Wikipedia note. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_employment_websites)

### Inferences
- Positioning: "the open successor to Jobboardfinder" with per fact citations, last verified dates, public JSON API, and an integration and ownership graph. Outreach to boards previously listed on Jobboardfinder is a natural acquisition channel.
- Non US coverage (EU, LATAM, Africa, APAC) and public employment services are weakly served by US review sites and are a differentiator.
- Publishing a methodology page and dated changelog would directly contrast with competitors' undated counts.

### Gaps
- Whether a buyer of Jobboardfinder's data or a successor exists was not found.

## 4. Open datasets the project could use or link

### Takeaway
Open structured sources for job boards are thin; the most usable are Wikidata (as a linking target), Wikipedia (as a backlink target), and public employment service rosters (WAPES, EURES). Unified API coverage lists are useful but proprietary.

### Cited Findings
- WAPES (World Association of Public Employment Services) publishes member contact details under a "Members" page, usable as a seed list of national public employment services. Source: [WAPES FAQ](https://wapes.org/faqs/?lang=en); [WAPES missions](https://wapes.org/wapes-missions/?lang=en)
- WAPES has regional affiliates such as AASEP (Africa). Source: [WAPES blog](https://wapes.org/blog/category/aasep-en/page/2/?lang=en)
- US public employment service context: American Job Centers and Wagner Peyser program listings from the Department of Labor. Source: [DOL](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/american-job-centers/wagner-peyser-program)
- Wikidata has a WikiProject Occupations and active HR modelling research, giving a place to add job board items with sameAs links. Source: [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:WikiProject_Occupations); [CEUR-WS](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4108/paper8.pdf)

### Inferences
- Adding Wikidata QIDs to entries and contributing cited rows back to Wikipedia's under sourced list could earn authority and links.
- EURES national contact lists, OpenCorporates company records and ILO lists were not checked but are likely candidates for ownership and public service fields.

### Gaps
- EURES, ILO, OpenCorporates and Crunchbase access terms were not verified in this pass.
- Wikidata class QID for "employment website" and its item count were not confirmed.
